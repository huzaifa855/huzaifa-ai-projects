# PIA Aviation AI Agent & Study Suite (EASA / PCAA Part-66 B1 & B2)

A comprehensive aviation engineering learning platform and AI assistant tailored for PIA (Pakistan International Airlines) and EASA/PCAA Part-66 licensing exams (B1.1 Airplane Turbine & B2 Avionics).

## 🚀 How to Run
1. **Option 1 (One Click)**: Double-click `Launch-App.bat`.
2. **Option 2 (Direct Browser)**: Double-click [index.html](file:///c:/Users/hkpro/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/my-app/pia-aviation-agent/index.html) to open in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.

## 📂 Folder Contents
- `index.html` - Complete interactive dashboard, study modes, exam interface & AI assistant UI.
- `style.css` - Modern aerospace glassmorphic UI styling with dark mode aesthetic.
- `app.js` - Complete application logic, MCQ bank, oral exam engine, flashcards & interactive AI agent.
- `assets/` - Aerospace images and logos.
- `Launch-App.bat` - Quick 1-click launcher.

## ✈️ Included Features & Modules
- **All 17 EASA / PCAA Modules**: Mathematics (M1), Physics (M2), Electrical Fundamentals (M3), Electronic Fundamentals (M4), Digital Techniques (M5), Materials & Hardware (M6), Maintenance Practices (M7), Basic Aerodynamics (M8), Human Factors (M9), Aviation Legislation (M10), Turbine Aeroplane Aerodynamics (M11A), Helicopter Aerodynamics (M12), Aircraft Aerodynamics/Structures/Systems (M13), Propulsion (M14), Gas Turbine Engine (M15), Piston Engine (M16), Propeller (M17).
- **Interactive MCQ Exam Engine**: Real Part-66 exam simulations with timer, pass/fail grading (75% passing criteria), review mode, and rationale explanations.
- **Oral / Viva Voice Practice**: Simulated oral examinations with model answers and evaluation.
- **Interactive Flashcards**: Quick-recall cards with mastery tracking.
- **Smart Study Planner**: Priority roadmap for high-yield topics and licensing tracks.
- **PIA Research & Systems Vault**: Deep-dives into PIA fleet airframes (Boeing 777-200ER/LR/300ER, Airbus A320-200), avionics, ATA chapters, hydraulics, fly-by-wire, engine systems (GE90, CFM56).
- **PIA AI Assistant Chat**: Real-time Q&A for troubleshooting, system logic, and concept clarification.
